## Procedure

1. Check the order date. Refunds are automatic within 30 days of purchase.
2. Between 30 and 90 days, a refund needs a stated reason. Record it.
3. Past 90 days, refuse politely and offer store credit instead.
4. Never refund more than the original amount. See `limits.md` for the
   per-tier caps.
5. State the expected time for the money to arrive: 5-10 business days.

## Tone

Be direct about the decision. Do not apologise repeatedly; say what will happen
and when.
